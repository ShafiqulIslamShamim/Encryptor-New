# Encryptor – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026
Contact: shafiqulislamshamimofficial@gmail.com

Encryptor respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

---

Intended Users

Encryptor is intended for users aged 16 and older, including developers, security-conscious users, and tech enthusiasts. The app is not designed for children.

---

Information We Collect

Encryptor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data such as your name, email, private encryption keys, or converted text content.

To ensure stability and diagnose technical issues, Encryptor collects anonymous diagnostic data and crash logs via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics as outlined in Section 6 below.

The app may also generate a local log file only when you manually enable logging, which remains on your device unless you explicitly share it.

---

Required Private Key

Encryptor requires a user-defined private key for encrypting and decrypting text in Encryption modes.

- This key is never stored on external servers or transmitted across networks.
- There is no login, user account, or remote database storing your keys.
- You are solely responsible for remembering your key to decrypt previously encrypted content.

---

Internet Access

Encryptor uses an internet connection to check for app updates and to transmit anonymous crash logs and stability metrics to Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics.

---

Third-Party Services & Analytics (Firebase)

Encryptor integrates Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (operated by Google LLC) to monitor application stability, analyze crash reports, and improve performance.

- Diagnostic Data Collected: Anonymous crash stack traces, device hardware model, Android OS version, app version, and aggregated usage metrics.
- Data Privacy & Security: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics DO NOT access, inspect, or record your private keys, plain text inputs, encrypted output, or personal identity.
- Other Libraries: Encryptor uses trusted open-source libraries (e.g., AndroidX, Material Components) for standard Android UI and app capabilities.

---

Data Sharing

Encryptor does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.  
Anonymous diagnostic data and crash stack traces are processed through Google Firebase services solely to fix bugs, prevent crashes, and enhance app performance.

---

Children’s Privacy

Encryptor is not intended for children under 16.  
We do not knowingly collect any personal data from minors.

---

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted within the app or on this page.
